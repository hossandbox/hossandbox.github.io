2. Split-Sleeper Calculation Logic (49 CFR § 395.1(g)(1))
The 2020 FMCSA rule update requires pairing two distinct qualifying breaks that together total at least 10 hours:
* Long Period: At least 7 consecutive hours in the Sleeper Berth (off-duty does not qualify).
* Short Period: At least 2 consecutive hours (can be Sleeper Berth, Off-Duty, or a combination).
* Neither period counts against the 14-hour driving window once the split is completed.
                PAIRING TIMELINE
=====================================================
... [Break 1 (e.g. 3h)] === [ Intermediate Driving & On-Duty ] === [Break 2 (e.g. 7h SB)] ...
        |                                                              |
        +------------- RE-CALCULATION WINDOW ANCHOR -------------------+
                      (Clock measures forward from here)

Calculation Algorithm:
1. Validate Pairing Condition: \text{Duration}(P_1) + \text{Duration}(P_2) \ge 10\text{ hours} \max(\text{Duration}_{\text{SB}}(P_1), \text{Duration}_{\text{SB}}(P_2)) \ge 7.0\text{ hours} \min(\text{Duration}(P_1), \text{Duration}(P_2)) \ge 2.0\text{ hours}
2. Anchor Point Shift:
   * When P_2 completes, the calculation anchor resets to the end of P_1.
   * All on-duty/driving events before the end of P_1 are cleared from the active shift window.
3. Remaining 11-Hour Drive Clock: \text{Drive}_{\text{available}} = 11.0 - \sum \text{Drive Hours between } \text{End}(P_1) \text{ and } \text{Start}(P_2)
4. Remaining 14-Hour Shift Window: \text{Window Used} = \left(\text{Current Time} - \text{End}(P_1)\right) - \text{Duration}(P_2) \text{Window}_{\text{available}} = 14.0 - \text{Window Used}
3. Rolling 70-Hour / 8-Day Recap Engine (49 CFR § 395.3(b))
A driver operating on an 8-day schedule cannot drive after logging 70 on-duty hours across any rolling 8 consecutive days.
Data Model:
An array of daily totals:
H = [h_7, h_6, h_5, h_4, h_3, h_2, h_1, h_0]
(where h_0 is today's current accumulated on-duty time, and h_7 is the oldest day in the 8-day window)
Real-Time Metrics:
* Current Cycle Used: \text{Cycle}_{\text{used}} = \sum_{i=0}^{7} h_i
* Available Driving Time Today: \text{Hours Available Today} = \min\left(11.0, \, (70.0 - \text{Cycle}_{\text{used}})\right)
* Hours Gained at Midnight (Drop-Off): At 00:00 tomorrow, day h_7 drops off the rolling record: \text{Recap Recovery} = h_7 \text{Available Tomorrow (before today's carryover)} = 70.0 - \left(\sum_{i=0}^{6} h_i\right)
34-Hour Restart Reset Flag:
If any single block of continuous Off-Duty/Sleeper Berth time \ge 34.0\text{ hours} is detected, clear all previous days:
\text{Cycle}_{\text{used}} = \sum \text{Hours since end of 34h reset}
4. "What-If" Trip Feasibility Engine
The planning feature allows a driver to simulate a prospective load without touching an ELD:
Inputs:
 - Planned Departure Time
 - Estimated Road Distance (miles)
 - Estimated Average Speed (default: 55 mph)
 - Estimated Shipper/Receiver Dwell (hours on-duty)

Outputs:
 - Estimated Drive Time: Distance / Speed
 - Required 30-Min Rest Stops: Triggered after 8 cumulative hours of driving
 - Required Night Stoppage / Split Berth Options
 - Projected 70-Hour Clock Balance on Day of Delivery

If estimated hours push \text{Cycle}_{\text{used}} > 70.0 or the shift duration > 14.0 before arrival, the UI flags the exact transit point where a mandatory 10-hour or split break must occur.
