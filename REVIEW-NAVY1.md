## Problems (non-blocking; fix in a follow-up)

- **P1. The A4 pins claim more than they enforce.** The handoff says A4 was "Verified in smoke.mjs (A4 rendering assertions)". The smoke block's comment names "Recap/Split (Stat tone tag)". In fact the block asserts only four things: `taxis-bind`, `live-badge`, `viol-ok` and the Trip `card-status-badge`. No test file references the following:
  - `stat-tone-tag`
  - `viol-glyph`
  - `mw-conflict-glyph`
  - the Split badge
  - `legal-quick`
  - `data-plan-group` / `plan-section-head`
  - `tool-sub`

  I confirmed this by mutation (smoke exit 0 with each one removed). **Fix:** add assertions for at least the Stat tag, the violation glyph, the Split badge and the legal strip. Also pin the group membership (`data-plan-group` → tool list), so the B1 reorganisation cannot silently drift. The re-aimed hub pins now check only that the titles are present.
- **P2. A stale order pin in c3browser.mjs.** It still asserts the old flat Plan order ("Hours coming back 4th … Miles at bottom") and now fails at 412 and 360. The builder re-aimed 9 other suites but did not run or re-aim this one. **Fix:** re-aim it to presence plus group, like `c3.mjs` D10a.
- **P3. The Plan tile subtitle clamp went from 3 lines to 2, so more plain-language text is hidden.** Measured with scrollHeight vs clientHeight:
  - at 412px, 7 of 12 subtitles are truncated (the baseline also truncated 7, but at 3 lines);
  - at 360px, **10 of 12** are truncated; "Flip My Clock" shows 34 of 101px and "Split-sleeper rescue" 34 of 84px.

  "Can I Legally Use It?" loses its list of exceptions. The handoff discloses this ("2-line subtitle clamp for brisk scanning"), and nothing becomes unreachable, but it works against the project's rule of plain-language explanations under trucker terms. **Recommendation:** go back to 3 lines, or remove the clamp on the full-width orphan tiles.

## Nits

- **N1. The handoff is wrong in several places.**
  - It says `index.html`, `manifest` and `store.ts applyTheme` were moved off carbon `#090c10`. The baseline already had `#06112e` in all three. The only real A1 change is the ground gradient.
  - It says lebrowser was "expanded from 2 to 6 viewports". The file is unchanged; geminifix4 already had 6.
  - Its storage-key list leaves out `hos-sandbox-undo-v1` (which is in fact unchanged).
  - It gives the legal pill labels as "Personal conveyance" / "16-hr big day". The code says **"Personal use"** / **"16-hr day"**.
- **N2. Undisclosed reorder in Now Shift actions.** End my day moved from 2nd to last (drift → Fix start → Exceptions → End my day). This is defensible ("the day's last act"), and every control remains. The long D6-A and D19 design-rationale comments were condensed to one line.
- **N3. "Personal use" is weaker wording than PC / Personal conveyance**, the regulatory term the card itself uses. Consider "PC / personal".
- **N4. The A2 pin is narrow.** It catches a literal `color:#0b5fa8` outside print, but not `fill`, `stroke`, or a token value. The token route is caught anyway by the contrast gate (the `--link:#0b5fa8` mutation → 2.62:1 FAIL). The A1 pin misses `--ground: …var(--raised-hi)…` and a direct `.app{background:#0d2a64}` (both mutations pass). Consider a computed-style check in a browser lane.
- **N5. The Settings beta-rules `<a>` has no styling.** It renders Chrome's default dark-scheme link colour `rgb(158,158,255)`, which is blue-violet on the amber Wind-down page. It passes contrast, and it was already unstyled in night. Give it `color: var(--link)`.
- **N6.** `mw-subline` lost its `mono` class (My Week verdict subline font). This is undisclosed and only presentational.
- **N7.** Carried over: the dead `@media (max-width: 340px)` rules at styles.css:632/653, and the layout/reimagine/c2browser wall-clock and stale failures, all present in the baseline.
