## New findings

### N1 — MEDIUM (already in work-logpatch, not added by this fix): a "Compare today" sheet left open across the carrier-day start compares the live ELD against the *old* day's end and offers a phantom fill
- **Where:** the window is captured at CTA tap (`app.tsx:4501`, `day: {start, end, label, back}`). `DriftSheet` then runs `driftCheck(getState(), eld, now, dayWindow)` with the live `now` (`app.tsx:3620`). `driftCheck` evaluates at `asOf = min(day.end, now)` (`store.ts:2801`). Once `now` passes `day.end`, Today's evaluation freezes at midnight while the ELD keeps counting.
- **Failure scenario (driven in the App, `mid-logpatch2.src.mjs` → `midnight-dom.log`):** Thu logged OFF to 12:00, D 12:00–15:00, an unlogged gap 15:00–16:00, OFF 16:00–21:00, live Driving since 21:00. At Thu 23:55 the driver taps "Compare today" and goes to grab the ELD screenshot. At Fri 00:20 they type the ELD's truthful 4h 40m.
  - Now Refresh: **sync**.
  - The open Log sheet (still titled "Comparing Today, Thu, Nov 26") says **"Your ELD shows 20m less driving left … isn't in the log yet"** and offers **Add the missing driving**. Tapping writes `D Thu 15:40–16:00 "Drift check"`, which never happened.
  - Pure store gives the same result (`midnight.mjs`) on both work-logpatch and work-logpatch2.
- **Why only MEDIUM:** it needs the sheet open across the carrier-day boundary. The phantom amount is bounded by drive (or 14h-window) time used after the boundary. The driver still has to tap, and the row is undoable and labelled. But it is the same class as F1/F2 (Log disagrees with Now, with a fill behind it) and it hits overnight drivers, so I'd fix it.
- **Fix direction:** for `back === 0`, don't freeze at the captured `day.end`. Either evaluate at `now` and recompute the fence from `carrierDayStart(now)` at compare time, or have the sheet refuse to compare (and ask to reopen) when `now >= day.end`.

### Observations (no action required)
- **O1:** On a past day, a *clocks* screenshot taken by mistake ("Drive 7:00 / Shift 9:00 / Cycle 50:00") is OCR'd as `Driving 7h 00m` (`extractEldDayTotals` accepts `drive` as a totals label). It's then reported as "3h 00m more driving". It is report-only with no write path, and the copy tells the driver to use the Daily Log. That's acceptable, but it is a misleading message in that misuse case. Requiring `driving`/`D`, not `drive`, in totals mode would tighten it.
- **O2:** `driftCheck(s, x, now, pastWindow)` still returns the end-of-day "fresh 11:00" value. Nothing calls it for past days now, but the doc comment is the only fence. A future caller could reintroduce F2(b).
- **O3:** In real Chromium, a rightward edge drag while a sheet is open dismisses the sheet (sheet back-gesture); the day and page don't change. Not a swipe misfire.
- **O4:** Under TZ=UTC with the device in America/Chicago the day title gains the two-zones subtitle; behaviour is otherwise identical in both zones.
